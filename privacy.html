Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Browsing the library needs no account and sets no tracking cookies. If you create an account (email sign-in or Google) or hold a subscription, we store your email address in our database to verify and grant your access, and the PayPal payer email to match your payment to your account. We do not use advertising pixels; any web analytics we enable are cookie-less.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal. We receive your name, email address and payment confirmation from PayPal in order to grant access and send receipts. We never see or store your full card or bank details.

What we use your data for

  • To provide subscription access to paid prompts.
  • To send transactional receipts and, if you opt in, occasional product updates.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We only share it with PayPal as needed to process payments and with service providers essential to running the service.

Retention

We keep subscription records for as long as your account is active or as needed to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. You can also cancel your subscription from your PayPal account.
