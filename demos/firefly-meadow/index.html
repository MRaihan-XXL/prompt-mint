The story

Small lights, patient dark.

Nothing here is a loop. Every spark keeps its own rhythm, and the whole field answers to wind, moon and you.

Out-of-phase blink

Each firefly drifts on its own noise path and pulses on its own clock, so the field shimmers instead of flashing in unison.

Wind in every blade

Tens of thousands of instanced blades bend on layered sine gusts, rooted to the ground and brightest at the tip where the moon catches.

Moon on the water

A crescent rises over the ridge and lays a column of glitter down the stream, rippling as the breeze moves across it.

Field notes

Built to stay light on its feet.

Grass, flowers and fireflies are each a single instanced draw. Pixel ratio is clamped, fog hides the distance, and everything pauses its motion if you ask your device for less.
