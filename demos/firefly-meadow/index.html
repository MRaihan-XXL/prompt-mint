The field

Three habits, one living meadow.

140drifters

Drifters

Slow wanderers steered by a curl-noise field, hovering between 0.3 and 1.8 m. They never cross, never stop, and fade out after 8–14 seconds only to be reborn elsewhere.

60chasers

Chasers

Curious ones. Bring your cursor within 2.5 m and they spring toward it at up to 1.2 m/s — the other twenty blink in sync the moment three of them meet.

4seasons

Seasons

Spring blossom, summer peak, autumn settling, winter dormancy. Fog, moonlight, grass, trees and flowers all morph over 2.5 seconds, with weather that belongs to each.

Notes from the field

Moonlight that shapes, not just lights.

One directional moon casts soft PCF shadows from eight trees across twelve thousand blades. Each blade is dark at its root and catches rim light at the tip, so the grass reads translucent instead of flat green.
