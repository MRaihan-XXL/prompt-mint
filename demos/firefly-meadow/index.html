01 — The field

Twelve thousand blades, one slow evening.

Every blade is its own instance with its own phase, lean and height; the tips whip further than the roots, the way real grass does. Above it, two hundred and twenty lights keep a rhythm none of them chose.

Scroll to walk in. Hover to call the chasers. Switch the season and the whole field re-tunes — fog, light, colour, the hour of the moon.
