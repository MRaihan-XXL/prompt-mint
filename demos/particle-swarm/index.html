01 — FORMATIONS

Three shapes, one swarm.

Every point owns a target in each formation. Swapping rewrites the targets in place — no allocation, no rebuild — and the points follow on their own clocks.

FIBONACCI · R 4.2

Sphere

A golden-angle lattice with ±0.35 radial jitter. The still point of the instrument, and the first thing the swarm assembles into.

P2 · Q3 · PATH 3.4 · TUBE 1.15

Torus knot

Points ride the knot by their index and spread around a 1.15 tube. The colour ramp reads along the path, cyan to gold.

3 STRANDS · R 3.2 · PITCH 5.5

Helix

Three interleaved strands, 120° apart, climbing from −1.6 to 4.4. The cloud turns into a spinning column of light.

02 — PHYSICS

Springs with a wave of delay.

Each point waits fract(seed)·0.45 s before its spring engages, so a swap sweeps through the cloud as a wave instead of a uniform snap. Two regimes change how hard it pulls and how long it rings.
