PulseOps

A SaaS landing page whose hero is a live dashboard. One canvas, eight metric lanes, real readouts, one clear action.

  • Base #070b14 · cyan #4cc9f0 · amber #ffb347
  • Lanes 8 × (H/8), 240-sample ring buffer, 1 sample / 100 ms
  • Signal clamped walk ±0.06, value-noise knot every 6 samples
  • p99 value mapped to 80–420 ms, printed at the last sample
  • Errors turn amber with halo above 0.75
  • Pulse ring 3→14 px, 0.8 s, alpha .4→0 when a sample beats its 20-sample mean
  • Scan 1 px line, alpha .08, 4 s top→bottom
  • Parallax lane group moves up scrollY × 0.08
  • Motion off reduced-motion freezes the stream on a static frame

Swap the brand, lane labels (LABELS) and the two accent colors in :root. Everything else follows.
