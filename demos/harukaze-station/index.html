01 · The Line

One platform, three cars, twelve seconds.

The 07 local eases in over 1.5 seconds, dwells for three, and rolls on. The crossing gates drop 2.6 seconds ahead of it — right on time, every loop.

LOOP

Arrive, board, depart. The cycle never drifts; the ETA chip counts it down live.

Commuter set with sliding doors and windows that light up from dusk.
