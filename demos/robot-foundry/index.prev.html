Build brief

  • PRNG mulberry32, 6-digit seed, reseeded every draw.
  • Palette base #0a0d12, amber #ffd166, cyan #4cc9f0, pink #f06292. Nothing else.
  • Head rounded-rect r40, square bevel 8, flat-top hexagon, capsule. w 200–300, h = w × 0.72–1.05, stroke 4px amber on #1a2030.
  • Eyes visor 120×18, twin dots r10, hollow rounds r14, thin bar 80×6. Centre y −18.
  • Mouth grille ×5 slots, smile arc, straight 80×4, none (30%). Centre y +40.
  • Morph parts spring from 0: stiffness 220, damping 16, 0.04s stagger per group.
  • Scan line 2px cyan at α0.25, 3.2s top to bottom, 0.4s pause on the eye line.
  • Robust dpr ≤ 2, adaptive down under 50fps, reduced-motion shows the face instantly.
