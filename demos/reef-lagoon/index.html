Water that actually answers

A 256² half-float height field runs at a fixed 60 Hz. Splashes, rain and fins all feed the same simulation.

256²Height field

Ping-pong half-float targets, velocity damping 0.9935, edge sponge 0.97.

60HzFixed step

Simulation is decoupled from render rate, so ripples look the same at any fps.

±0.004Chromatic split

Refraction bends the sand and splits red and blue at the ripple crests.

14Creature cap

One instanced mesh per species, zero allocation in the update loop.

  1. Caustics — 256² tiling texture scrolling at (0.02, 0.013).
  2. Floor — sand fBm, coral heads or kelp, rendered offscreen.
  3. Creatures — instanced bodies with max-blended blob shadows.
  4. Water surface — refraction, fresnel sky, sun glint, depth tint, foam.
  5. Overlay — above-water parts, splash rings, droplets, rain.

Four controls, four rewrites

The bar at the top right is not decoration. Each switch changes light, geometry, weather or camera.

Theme

Lagoon Noon, Golden Dusk, Biolume Night. Repaints sun, water tint, sand and the whole interface.

Floor dressing

Swap the coral garden for swaying kelp channels. Different geometry, not a recolour.

Weather

A squall adds 800 rain streaks, dims the sun to 55% and lifts wave amplitude by 40%.

Layout

Drop from the 26-unit overview to a tilted shallows view; the dock grows live stat cards.
