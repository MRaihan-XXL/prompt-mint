How it’s made

Everything is procedural: a 400×400 heightfield with CPU-baked horizon AO, merged into one vertex-coloured mesh for the architecture and one emissive mesh for the glow, plus roughly five thousand instanced canopy blobs.

The scene renders into an MSAA half-float target with a depth texture. The water is a separate pass that reads that depth to get real thickness (Beer–Lambert), refracts, ray-marches screen-space reflections and adds foam from a terrain height map. Ripples come from a 256² wave-equation simulation stepped at 60 Hz.
