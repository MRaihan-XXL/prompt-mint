01 / MELT

Melt

The tower furnace holds the charge at 1,240°C behind a sliding blast door. A tap chute feeds the ladle, so the metal is always staged before the mold arrives.

02 / CAST

Cast

The ladle tilts 0.9 rad over 1.6 s and pours a 6 cm stream into the waiting mold. The car then rides the roller conveyor at 0.55 m/s while the ingot ramps from white-hot to dull red.

03 / STACK

Stack

At x = 0.6 m the stamping arm winds up for 0.4 s and strikes in 0.12 s. At the end of the line the gantry crane clamps the cooled ingot and stacks it three high, two columns.
