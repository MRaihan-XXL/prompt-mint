01 / Research

Three ways to read a sky.

Every night on the ridge produces three records. Together they turn light into a dataset you can cite.

01 · Curtain

Curtain

Five ribbons tracked at 64 vertical slices each. Height, sway and brightness are logged every 200 ms so a single arc becomes a time series.

02 · Drift

Drift

Curtains never move in lockstep. We fit independent drift rates to each one, then compare them against solar-wind speed from the same hour.

03 · Signal

Signal

Photometers at 557.7, 630.0 and 427.8 nm turn colour into counts. The sky becomes a spectrum instead of a postcard.

02 / Stations

Three ridges, one horizon.

Each station sits above the tree line with a clear view north and no light within forty kilometres.

  • 01

    Ridge North

    68.21°N · 19.42°E · 612 m

  • 02

    Frozen-lake mirror rig — reflections give us the lower edge of every curtain.
