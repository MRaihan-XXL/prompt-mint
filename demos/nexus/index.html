The brief

NEXUS One is a product landing page built around a live 3D headset in <model-viewer> with an Iron Man-style HUD overlay.

  • Pointer: yaw ±15°, pitch ±10°, 7% lerp per frame.
  • Idle 2.2 s: a ±2.5° sine sway fades in over 1.2 s.
  • Click the hero: a 360° spin, 1.4 s, cubic in-out.
  • HUD: Off / Scan (rings, reticle, brackets, sweeps) / Combat (+ threats, status).
  • Drones: None / Orbit (2) / Swarm (6 with scan beams).
  • Mech: Stowed / Deploying (4 arms, bars) / Active (jitter, claws, holo panels).
  • Environment: Studio / Night / Void / Grid.
  • Scroll: the hero pins, the model eases to 0.85 and the spec strip rises.
