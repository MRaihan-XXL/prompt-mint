Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Digital goods

DepthGlow sells digital prompt content delivered instantly on subscription. Because the product is available immediately, all sales are generally final.

When we do refund

We want you to be happy. We will issue a full refund if:

  • You were charged in error or a duplicate payment was taken.
  • You subscribed but could not access the paid prompts and we were unable to fix it.
  • You request a refund within 7 days of your first payment and have not downloaded or copied a substantial number of paid prompts.

How to request a refund

Contact us with your PayPal transaction ID within 14 days of the charge. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via PayPal, typically within 5–10 business days.

Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription anytime from your PayPal account. Cancellation stops future charges; you keep access until the end of the current billing period. We do not pro-rate partial periods.
