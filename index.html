FAQ

Everything you need to know before subscribing.

What is DepthGlow?

DepthGlow is a library of copy-paste prompts that make AI tools (Claude, Cursor, v0, Bolt, Lovable) generate cinematic 2D and real-time 3D hero sections, landing pages and full sites — without the generic AI look.

Which AI tools do these prompts work with?

Any text-to-site or text-to-code AI that accepts a long-form prompt. Tested with Claude, Cursor, v0, Bolt, Lovable and GPT-4o. The prompts are tool-agnostic — paste wherever you work.

Can I use the prompts for client or commercial projects?

Yes. Every paid plan (Pro and Max) includes a commercial license. You can use the generated sites for your own products, client work, or anything you sell. Free prompts are for personal / evaluation use only.

How do I access Pro or Max prompts after subscribing?

Sign in with the same email you used on PayPal. The Pro and Max templates unlock automatically — copy buttons activate and full prompt text becomes visible.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed entirely through PayPal. Cancel anytime at paypal.com/myaccount/autopay. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Do I get new templates after subscribing?

New drops ship weekly. Pro members see every new 2D and standard 3D template. Max members get priority access to premium high-detail 3D and shader prompts first.
